Turn your teaching material into a localized, theory-grounded interactive tool

Choose your learners, setting and educational approach. The assistant writes original content for each unit, checks it automatically, and builds one offline HTML file with games, puzzles, reading, listening, grammar, writing and a teacher guide — the same engine as Mindset Companion.

Subjects: English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, General Science and Islamic Education, for all school stages and university. All content is original; your material is used only to understand the topic, vocabulary focus and level. No sign-in needed. Generation takes about 1–3 minutes per unit.
